# Human Oversight Design — TalentScreen (fictional)

**Status:** Proposed workflow. No actual reviewer, interface, training or decision record exists.

Meaningful oversight requires a competent reviewer to understand system limitations, inspect source evidence, have adequate time and authority, and be able to correct, override or escalate. A click-through or signature is not sufficient evidence.

## Workflow
1. Clearly label AI-generated outputs and display model/criteria versions, uncertainty and missing fields.
2. Link material extracted fields to exact source passages.
3. Require independent assessment against job-related criteria; do not present rank as a decision.
4. Record corrections with old/new value, source, reviewer and timestamp.
5. Permit queue override and rejection of AI recommendations.
6. Escalate ambiguous, conflicting or potentially discriminatory cases.
7. Define applicant correction/complaint routes subject to applicable law.
8. Monitor override patterns, unsupported rationales and group outcomes where lawful.

## Proposed accountabilities
System owner: purpose, approval and pause authority. HR process owner: criteria and workflow. Recruiter: independent review and correction. Model owner: versions and validation. Privacy/legal: applicability and rights. Security: access and incidents. Independent assurance: challenge and evidence review. All roles are placeholders, not actual appointments.

## Tests required
Seed extraction errors and verify detection; trace every material field to a source; test override and escalation; assess reviewer understanding; inspect review and correction logs. No test has been run. This design does not prove effective oversight or legal compliance.
