## Safety and governance interpretation

These records are useful as **content-policy and output-boundary evaluation evidence**. They show the Arena evaluator's displayed assessment of the model's responses in the named challenge scenarios. They do not constitute a legal determination that a particular work was protected in every jurisdiction, nor an independent analysis of licensing, fair use/fair dealing or infringement.

A comparable AI service should define content-reproduction policy, rights-aware output constraints, safe alternatives, escalation/appeal paths and measurable tests. Legal and licensing conclusions require jurisdiction- and work-specific review.
