## Case 01 — Different errors, comparable impact

**User request**

A shop sold 8 notebooks at ₦1,250 each and 5 pens at ₦400 each. Calculate the total revenue. Show the two subtotals and the final total.

**Response A**

Notebooks: 8 × ₦1,250 = ₦10,000  
Pens: 5 × ₦400 = ₦2,000  
Total revenue: ₦12,500.

**Response B**

Notebooks: 8 × ₦1,250 = ₦10,000  
Pens: 5 × ₦400 = ₦2,500  
Total revenue: ₦12,500.

**Your task:** Compare the severity and practical effect of the arithmetic errors. Do not assume the matching final totals make either response correct.

## Case 02 — Correct facts, failed instruction

**User request**

Using only the information below, summarize the project status in **exactly three bullet points**. Do not add recommendations.

**Source information**

- Design is complete.
- Implementation is 60% complete.
- Testing has not started.
- The planned release date is 30 June.

**Response A**

- Design is complete.
- Implementation is 60% complete; testing has not started.
- Release is planned for 30 June.

**Response B**

- Design is complete.
- Implementation is 60% complete.
- Testing has not started.
- Release is planned for 30 June.

**Your task:** Decide whether factual correctness compensates for violating an explicit formatting constraint.

## Case 03 — Subtle unsupported detail

**User request**

Summarize this policy excerpt in one sentence.

**Policy excerpt**

“Employees may request remote work for up to two days per week, subject to manager approval. Requests must be submitted through the HR portal.”

**Response A**

Employees may request up to two remote-work days per week with manager approval, and must submit requests through the HR portal.

**Response B**

Employees may request up to two remote-work days per week with manager approval, and must submit requests through the HR portal at least 48 hours in advance.

**Your task:** Identify whether every detail in each summary is supported by the excerpt. A detail can sound plausible and still be invented.

## Case 05 — Correct answer, invalid reasoning

**User request**

A report states that a town had 100 reported cases in January and 80 reported cases in February. What was the percentage decrease from January to February? Show the calculation.

**Response A**

The decrease was 20 cases.  
Percentage decrease = (20 ÷ 100) × 100 = 20%.  
So reported cases decreased by 20%.

**Response B**

The decrease was 20 cases.  
Percentage decrease = (20 ÷ 80) × 100 = 25%.  
The decrease was 20%, because 20 fewer cases were reported out of the original 100.  
So the percentage decrease was 20%.

**Your task:** Evaluate the validity and consistency of the explanation, not just whether the final percentage is correct.
