## Evidence boundaries
All examples are self-directed. Datasets are synthetic unless a source is explicitly identified. They are not paid client work, proprietary production data or independently validated benchmark results. Scores against author-created references do not establish independent proficiency. Independent annotator agreement and candidate production throughput remain unmeasured. The production-style assessment pack is prepared but has not been completed by the candidate; its public reference key makes it an honor-system exercise, not a secure blind benchmark.

Governance framework mappings are thematic and evidence-scoped; they do not establish implementation, conformity, certification or legal compliance. The governance case is fictional and its simulation uses deliberately seeded synthetic evidence. No real control implementation, operating effectiveness or independent audit is claimed. The EU AI Act analysis is conditional, not legal advice.

The free-model security evaluation is preliminary, uses dynamic routing and synthetic tools, and is not a statistically valid benchmark. Its repeated-trial ledger remains a plan until trials are actually run and raw evidence preserved.

## Method
Parse instructions; identify evidence and unknowns; apply a documented rubric; record source-grounded rationale; flag ambiguity; validate structure; document limitations and corrective actions.
