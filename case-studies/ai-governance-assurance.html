IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

What is AI governance?

AI governance is how an organization decides who is responsible for an AI system, what risks it must manage, what checks are required and what evidence shows those checks are working. A written policy is not proof that a control operates; the evidence has to show what was actually done.

01

Identify the risk

Who could be harmed, what could go wrong and how serious would the impact be?

02

Define the control

Specify the people, process or technical safeguard intended to reduce that risk.

03

Check the evidence

Look for records showing the control exists, is used and achieves its intended purpose.

FRAMEWORK APPLICATION

Evidence-scoped mapping

The repository maps selected themes to NIST AI RMF 1.0 and ISO/IEC 42001:2023 and provides conditional EU AI Act issue analysis. It does not claim NIST conformance, ISO certification, legal determination or operating control effectiveness.

ASSURANCE CONCLUSION

Documentation is not implementation

The TalentScreen design exercise demonstrates structured governance documentation and evaluator behavior against seeded synthetic records. It does not establish real-system deployment, fairness, performance, control operation or independent assurance. No assurance opinion is expressed.
