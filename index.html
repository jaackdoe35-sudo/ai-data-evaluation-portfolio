06 / Evidence policy

Honest about what
the evidence proves.

The portfolio is designed to make its limitations as inspectable as its findings.

The work shown is self-directed unless explicitly stated otherwise. Gray Swan Arena metrics are a dated profile snapshot supplied by the researcher and may change. Competition results apply to the specific challenge criteria and do not establish general model vulnerability. Promptfoo Project 20 contains 12 test specifications; its repeated-trial ledger is a plan, not completed trials. Preliminary free-model tests used dynamic routing and synthetic tools and are not a benchmark or production security assessment. The AI governance case study is fictional; proposed controls and simulations do not establish implementation, legal compliance, certification or independent assurance. Annotation and response-evaluation datasets are synthetic unless a source is identified. No paid client engagements, professional certifications, independent benchmark performance or production operating experience are claimed.

The full research repositories preserve the detailed prompts, transcripts, screenshots where available, evaluation records, methods, corrections and limitations. Where raw evidence is incomplete, that limitation is stated rather than inferred away.
