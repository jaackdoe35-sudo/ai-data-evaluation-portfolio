MY PROCESS

From question to evidence

A repeatable workflow that makes results easier to inspect and reproduce.

01

Define

Set the research question, scope and success criteria.

02

Design

Choose test cases and define expected observations.

03

Execute

Run controlled tests and preserve raw outputs.

04

Evaluate

Compare observations against explicit criteria.

05

Report

Document findings, evidence and limitations.

EVIDENCE STANDARDS

Work you can inspect

No invented testimonials or inflated claims. The emphasis is on traceable artifacts and transparent limitations.

01

Primary records

Prompts, test conditions, outputs and relevant screenshots are preserved where disclosure is permitted.

02

Clear evaluation

Results are tied to stated criteria and official evaluator outcomes where available.

03

Honest boundaries

Unverified claims, synthetic work samples and known limitations are identified rather than presented as proven results.
