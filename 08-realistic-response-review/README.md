## Summary

The participant's final pairwise preferences agreed with the author-created reference key in 6 of 6 cases after revising the initial C02 choice from A to B upon reviewing the damaged-item shipping-refund exception.

The participant also rated both responses across five dimensions: factuality and source fidelity, instruction-following, completeness, clarity and usability, and judgment and calibration. The reflection documents the scores, confidence report, decision revision, qualitative observations, and limitations.

## Scope and limitations

All scenarios and data are synthetic. The reference key is an author-created discussion guide, not an independently adjudicated gold standard. The scores and preference agreement are self-directed practice evidence only; they are not a validated proficiency benchmark, production performance measure, or evidence of paid professional work.
